# 2026-06-28 — an "it's been done" asserted without a search

The rule this produced is **Search before claiming novelty or prior art**, in
[global.md](../global.md).

Asserted the contribution was "moderate novelty, hypergraph IRs already exist" without
a search. A 4-query search then found the nearest works were the user's *own* precursor
(Hajdu & Hegyi 2025) and HyperGraphOS — neither pre-empting the claim — and surfaced two
must-cite related works that were missing from the draft.

The failure was not the conclusion, it was the order: the assertion came first and the
evidence never came at all. This is the literature-side of the same defect as naming a
"most likely cause" that was never isolated.
