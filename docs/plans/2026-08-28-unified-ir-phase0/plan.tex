\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Phase 0 is design and measurement only; no production source was modified.
This is the plan bundle. Evidence lives in
\code{reports/2026-08-28-unified-ir-phase0-audit.md}; the target architecture
in \code{docs/unified-ir-architecture.md}; the migration in
\code{docs/unified-ir-migration.md}. This document commits to the work rather
than restating the findings.

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{One structural fact drives the plan.} \code{Ir} keeps five arrays that
must grow in lockstep on every declaration insert, nothing in the type system
couples them, every reader indexes densely --- and \code{bridge.rs} in a
\emph{second repository} re-implements that lockstep by hand. Two writers of an
unenforced invariant, in two repositories. This is safe only while the IR is
immutable, which is exactly the property the target removes. The argument rests
on counts and sizes, not on timings.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, and the measurement condition}

\paragraph{Goal.} One canonical, mutable HyMeKo IR in \code{hymeko\_core}: the
parser's output, the emitter's input, the query and graph/algebra source, the
Python-owned model, the tensor source of truth, and the IR-stream transaction
target --- with COO/CSR/incidence/Laplacians/P-graphs/GPU resources as derived,
\emph{version-tagged} views rather than parallel truths.

\paragraph{Out of scope for Phase 0.} Replacing IDs, modifying \code{Ir}
storage, removing \code{HyMeKoEditorIR}, changing bridges, implementing
transactions. Multi-writer CRDT semantics are out of scope for the whole
migration as specified; the first target is single-writer, many-replica.

\paragraph{Measurement condition (binding).} All Phase~0 timings were collected
while an unrelated process (PID 4196, a \code{shuttle\_trainer} busy-wait) was
consuming a core. \textbf{They are contaminated and diagnostic only}, retained
in Appendix~I of the audit and never quoted as baselines. PID 4196 is
\textbf{not} to be terminated without separate explicit authorisation, and has
not been. No performance claim in this plan rests on those numbers.

\section{Sequencing: why there is no flag day}

\code{hymeko\_core} is a leaf; six crates in four repositories depend on it
one-way at compile time. A naive ID change breaks all of them in the same
instant --- 196 dense-index sites, 23 FFI sites and a published JSON DTO, in one
synchronised commit.

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{The move: separate encapsulation from representation.} Converting
\code{ir.decl\_nodes[d.0]} into \code{ir.decl\_node(d)} is
\emph{behaviour-preserving under the current representation}. So encapsulate
first, while nothing changes; then change the representation behind the
encapsulation, touching zero call sites.

\medskip
\noindent
This makes M1 --- the widest, riskiest stage --- \textbf{core-local}. The
resulting order rests on that argument alone and requires no measurement:

\paragraph{M2c is a safety gate, not cleanup.} While defect D4 stands
(\code{pub .0}, \code{From<usize>}), a representation change still
\emph{compiles} at any missed site and then indexes the wrong element --- a
silent wrong answer. Removing D4 converts every missed site into a compile
error. \textbf{M1 must not begin until M2c is green.}

\section{No wall-clock estimate is given}

The earlier draft gave day figures per stage. They are withdrawn. They rested
partly on contaminated timings and partly on an unknown the audit cannot close:
how fast 127 call sites migrate in a repository Phase~0 was forbidden to build
or test. The stage \emph{ordering} and the \emph{gates} are the plan. A schedule
requires, first, a quiet baseline (audit \S I.5) and one measured pilot
repository --- \code{hymeko\_hnn}, at six sites, is the natural pilot.

Publishing a confident schedule on those inputs would be the same error as
publishing the contaminated timings as baselines.

\section{Test strategy}

\begin{description}\itemsep3pt
\item[Unit] \code{Ir::validate()} against hand-built IRs, one test per
  invariant, each with a deliberately corrupted input that must be caught:
  length drift on each lockstep array, \code{decl\_to\_node} disagreeing with
  \code{NodeRec.decl}, \code{EdgeRec.arcs} disagreeing with
  \code{ArcRec.in\_edge}, a \code{next\_sibling} cycle.
\item[Regression (M0.1)] A batch whose third delta fails must leave the IR
  bit-identical to its pre-batch state. \emph{Fails against today's
  implementation} --- that is the point.
\item[Regression (M1)] A serialized \code{NodeId} must not deserialize as an
  \code{EdgeId}. Fails against today's untagged encoding.
\item[Correctness (M0.5)] Canonical-hash invariance under permuted editor
  construction history. \textbf{Needs no quiet host}; can land immediately. If
  it fails, M3's ordering assumptions need rework \emph{before}, not after.
\item[Round-trip (M5 gate)] \code{arcs.len()} asserted across
  arena$\to$editor$\to$arena. The current test asserts nodes and edges and
  omits precisely the quantity that collapses.
\item[Property (\code{proptest})] Random delta sequences preserve every
  \code{validate()} invariant; stage-then-abort is the identity on the base IR.
\item[Integration] Parser $\to$ lowering $\to$ \code{Ir} $\to$
  \code{emit\_hymeko} preserves \code{bases} and \code{AnnoR}. Absent today,
  which is why the bridge's loss went unnoticed.
\item[Performance] \code{ir\_bench.rs} on a \textbf{quiet} host, with ambient
  load recorded, competing variants \textbf{alternated} not blocked.
\end{description}

\section{Performance budget --- deferred, deliberately}

No budget is stated. Every candidate baseline is contaminated, and
\code{CLAUDE.md} \S3 forbids treating single-shot or contaminated numbers as
measurements. The budget is set from the first quiet run (audit \S I.5), which
must also \emph{extend} coverage: lookup, clone, canonical hash, binary
serialization, bridge crossings, \code{HyperGraphView::from\_ir} and COO/CSR ---
none of which \code{ir\_bench.rs} measures today.

Two size facts are \textbf{not} contaminated and stand as budget inputs:
derived mappings cost 61.1\% of the record tables at 20\,001 declarations, and
\code{size\_of(Option<NodeId>)} is 16 against \code{size\_of(NodeId)} of 8 ---
a niche-carrying \code{Id} takes the three \code{decl\_to\_*} maps from
960\,048\,B to 240\,012\,B.

\section{Rollback}

Each stage is independently revertable; that is why they are split rather than
merged. M0 items are additive or one-line. M2a is deletable. M2b reverts per
repository. M2c restores two \code{From} impls, and its failure mode is loud.
M2d's removed maps are derivable by construction, with \code{Ir::validate()}
proving the restoration. M1 is one commit \emph{because} M2b/M2c localised it.
M5 restores the bridge.

\section{Risk anticipation}

\paragraph{Performance-contract preservation.} \code{ir\_bench.rs} did not exist
before Phase~0; there was no baseline to preserve, which is why it was written
first --- and why its output is quarantined as contaminated rather than adopted.
The IR-stream side already carries contracts (bounded staged bytes, O(1) replay
lookup, fixed-capacity window); M4 attaches to that machinery and must inherit
them. \textbf{The Phase~8 rule applies verbatim: exactly one implementation of
the state machine.} This is also why M0.1 fixes \code{Batch}'s documentation
rather than building a second transaction mechanism four stages early.

\paragraph{Worst-case input.} Production scale is taken as $10^5$ declarations.
A \code{u32} payload caps declarations at $\approx 4.29\times10^9$ --- state it
as an explicit contract, or use \code{NonZeroU64} and forgo the niche saving.
Above $10^6$, or at per-keystroke/live-inference commit rates, full-state
identity stops being sufficient (\S1); the Merkle-fold digest specified in the
architecture keeps the incremental path reachable without rework.

\paragraph{The risk that is not in \code{core}.} 127 of 196 dense-index sites
are in \code{transformation}, which Phase~0 was forbidden to modify and has
therefore \textbf{not built or tested}. \code{bridge.rs} is additionally a
\emph{writer} of core's invariants. This is the largest unknown, and no work
inside \code{core} reduces it --- only the M2b pilot will.

\paragraph{Aspirational comments.} Before editing \code{ir.rs}, grep for
comments asserting invariants the code does not enforce. The five-array lockstep
is exactly such an unenforced convention, and \code{\#[repr(C)] // Stable layout
for serde and FFI} is an outright false one.
