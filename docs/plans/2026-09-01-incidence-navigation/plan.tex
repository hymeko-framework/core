\section*{Scope}
Core-only (\texttt{hymeko/core}, base \texttt{3190097} = core main after the U2.2
flip). Branch \texttt{feat/core-incidence-navigation}. Add the missing
\emph{node $\to$ incidence} direction to the canonical \texttt{Ir} navigation so
\texttt{hymeko\_graph} algorithms can traverse the hypergraph directly, without
materialising a SignedGraph / CSR / dense / star / clique view.

\section*{Decision (Stage 3 benchmark first)}
\texttt{proto\_incidence\_bench.rs} models the canonical incidence (edge $\to$ arc
$\to$ ref $\to$ target) and measures Berge-reachability three ways, so the full
cost (build $+$ search $+$ memory) is visible:
\begin{longtable}{lrrr}\toprule
metric & REV (reverse index) & DENSE (star CSR) & note \\\midrule
build & baseline & $1.5$--$7.1\times$ slower & DENSE quadratic in arity \\
bytes/incidence & $4.8$--$12$ & $16.8$--$78$ ($2$--$13\times$) & DENSE lossy \\
search & baseline & $-42\%$ .. $+41\%$ & parity; DENSE wins pure reachability, REV wins high arity \\
end-to-end & baseline & $1.0$--$2.3\times$ slower & REV wins \\
no-index scan & --- & --- & $\sim 650\times$ REV ($O(VE)$, unusable) \\\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\textbf{The reverse-incidence index is justified} (cheaper build, far less
memory, lossless, end-to-end faster); the no-index scan is unusable, so
node-major traversal needs it. It is added as a \emph{derived} index, not stored
state.

\section*{Representation}
\texttt{IncidenceIndex} — a compact \textbf{CSR} (\texttt{DeclId $\to$ incident
HyperArcIds}), \texttt{build\_incidence\_index(\&Ir) -> Result<\_, IncidenceError>}
(two passes, $O(\text{incidences})$, one alloc per array), and
\texttt{incident\_arcs(node) -> \&[HyperArcId]} (total, allocation-free). Lossless
(sign/weight reachable through the arc; duplicate incidences preserved);
canonical IDs in and out; \textbf{no} pairwise/star/clique/Levi expansion, no
implicit dedup. Caller-owned, built once and reused --- never rebuilt per query,
never stored in the serde wire. Typed errors: \texttt{TargetOutOfRange},
\texttt{IncidenceOverflow}. \texttt{index\_matches\_ir} is the opt-in coherence
gate (not a hot-path tax).

\section*{Why derived, not stored (Stage 5)}
The current \texttt{Ir} is append-only (no mutation/transaction), so a stored
index would only duplicate what is $O(\text{incidences})$-derivable and would add
serde/consistency burden for no benefit; the algorithm layer builds it once per
\texttt{Ir} and reuses it. The transaction-updated stored form for a future
mutable IR is Stage 14 (next step).

\section*{V\&V}
Golden shapes (multiway $+/-/\sim$, duplicate incidence, self-reference,
multi-arc-per-edge, same node in many edges, empty/singleton); an
\emph{independent} $O(VE)$ scan oracle (no index); a metamorphic reachability
check (reverse-index BFS $\equiv$ edge-major scan BFS); a dangling-target fault.
112 lib $+$ 210 integration $+$ doctests green; fmt, clippy \texttt{-D warnings}
clean.
