\paragraph{Default-off execution.} \texttt{test = false} keeps ordinary
\texttt{cargo test} from building the target. Additionally \texttt{main} returns

\texttt{--benches}/\texttt{--all-targets} test-mode invocations. The only launch is
\texttt{cargo bench --manifest-path <core>/Cargo.toml -p hymeko\_core --bench
circular\_buffer\_bench --offline}, from the user's Temp directory with
\texttt{CARGO\_TARGET\_DIR=<core>/target}.

\paragraph{Workload.} Payload \texttt{u64} = producer index shifted by 40 bits or
sequence. Implementations: Core \texttt{CircularBuffer} (Crossbeam
\texttt{ArrayQueue}) and a bounded \texttt{Mutex<VecDeque>} with the same
rejecting push, evicting force\_push and FIFO pop contract; a sequential parity
preflight compares both against each other before any timing. Modes:
\emph{rejecting} (push retried with a fixed spin-then-yield backoff until accepted)
and \emph{overwrite} (force\_push; every evicted value returned and counted).
Capacities 1, 64, 1024. Producers = consumers in \{1, 2, 4, 8\}; 8P/8C only when
available parallelism is at least 17.

\paragraph{Round.} One persistent worker pool per cell; threads are spawned once and
wait on a start barrier between rounds, outside timing. Timed window: start-barrier
release to the last consumer's completion. Producers split $N$ items; a consumer
stops only on an empty pop that began after it observed the producers-done flag.

\paragraph{Accounting (every round, warm-up included).} Inserted $= N$. Rejecting:
removed $= N$, evicted $= 0$. Overwrite: removed $+$ evicted $= N$, rejected $= 0$.
Wrapping sum and SplitMix64 checksum of inserted values equal those of removed and
evicted values; buffer empty after the round. Rejected pushes and empty pops are
counted separately and never enter a throughput numerator.

\paragraph{Throughput.} Criterion \texttt{iter\_custom}; one iteration is one
produced item. Flat sampling, 1 s warm-up, 10 samples over 2 s (about 200 ms each).
Per sample: transferred items/s (rejecting) or offered and delivered items/s
(overwrite), reported as median, IQR and worst. Criterion \texttt{sample.json}
iterations and times must equal the sidecar rounds exactly.

\paragraph{Latency.} Separate pass with the same pool and a sampled probe. Every
8th buffer call per thread is timed with RDTSC calibrated against \texttt{Instant}
(the Windows QPC resolution is 100 ns), classified by operation and outcome
(accepted/rejected push, inserting/evicting force\_push, removed/empty pop) into a
log-linear histogram (16 sub-buckets per octave, at most 6.25\% bucket width) with
exact maximum. One warm-up and 5 measured repetitions of $2^{18}$ items: p50, p90,
p99, p99.9 and maximum, median/IQR/worst over repetitions; instrumentation floor
reported.

\paragraph{Budgets.} Peak RSS below 1 GiB (fixed buffers, about 8 KiB per
operation class per thread). Measured wall at most 25 minutes. No overnight run.

\paragraph{Risks.} Hybrid P/E cores and Windows scheduling without affinity;
Balanced power plan; background processes are recorded, not killed; the backoff
policy determines failed-attempt counts; the latency probe perturbs contention;
worker wake-up latency lies inside the timed window (tens of microseconds against
200 ms samples). Results do not generalize to other hosts, payloads or schedulers.

no library behavior changes.
